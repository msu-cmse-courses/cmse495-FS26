# Course Milestone Roadmap

## Core Idea

You are building one project across communication, technical development, and software practice.

{% include mini-schedule.html keyword="MILESTONE" title="Milestone Timeline" %}

---

## Deadline Cadence

- All milestones are shared in Week 1
- One milestone is due every Sunday
- Milestones are due on time with no extensions

With good planning, teams should have enough time to complete work before deadlines.

---

## Typical Milestone Arc

The schedule has varied milestone labels by date, but the arc is consistent:

1. Project preferences and organization
2. Planning and early communication artifacts
3. Proposal/storyboard/video checkpoints
4. Outreach and install/reproducibility work
5. MVP and report drafts
6. Final storyboard and final project integration

## How To Use This Page

- Use this roadmap for the big picture
- Use the daily schedule for exact due dates and prompts

## Weekly System

Milestones work together with:

- [Weekly-3x3](Weekly-3x3) for reflection and alignment

## Bottom Line

Milestones are not separate tasks; they are structured checkpoints that keep teams communicating, building, and adjusting all semester.
