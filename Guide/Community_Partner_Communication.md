# Community Partner Communication

Community partner communication is part of the project work, not separate from it.

## Cadence

- Establish a regular update rhythm
- Send agenda/context before partner meetings when possible
- Send concise follow-up notes with decisions and next actions

## Professional Expectations

- Communicate early if scope, timing, or blockers change
- Ask clarifying questions instead of assuming
- Keep communication clear, respectful, and documented

## Compliance and Sharing

Before sharing content externally, confirm alignment with NDA/IP/FERPA/copyright requirements.

If project materials are intended for public release, get explicit team and partner approval first.

## Coordination With Team Systems

Partner communication should align with your internal systems:

- [Meeting-Agenda_and_Notes](Meeting-Agenda_and_Notes)
- [Milestone-Roadmap](Milestone-Roadmap)
