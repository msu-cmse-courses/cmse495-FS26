# Team Charter

The Team Charter is your team’s living operating agreement.

## Purpose

A strong charter helps teams:

- communicate clearly
- define shared expectations
- address conflict early using agreed norms

## Core Principle

Successful teams do two things: solve the project and build the working relationship.

## Required Sections

1. Team members and role coverage
2. Team purpose and success criteria
3. Expected outcomes for current phase
4. Communication norms (tools, response expectations, escalation)
5. Ground rules for common failure points

## Ground Rules Should Cover

- late or missed meetings
- missed deadlines
- unclear ownership
- disagreement and frustration
- how/when to ask instructors for help

## Use It As A Living Document

Review and revise the charter as your project evolves. When challenges arise, refer to expectation vs observation and update your working agreement.
