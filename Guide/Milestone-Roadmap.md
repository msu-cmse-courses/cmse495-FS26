# Course Milestone Roadmap  
## CMSE / Data Science / Software Engineering Projects

### Core Idea

Throughout this course, you are developing your project across three connected areas:

- **Communication** → explaining your work clearly  
- **Technical Development** → exploring and building your solution  
- **Software Practice** → organizing, sharing, and reproducing your work  

These evolve together over time.

---

## Project Phases

### Phase 1: Team Formation and Setup (Weeks 1–2)

Focus:
- forming your team  
- selecting a project  
- setting up your workflow  

Milestones:
- Project Preferences  
- Project Organization  

---

### Phase 2: Understanding and Framing (Weeks 2–4)

Focus:
- exploring the data  
- understanding the problem  
- forming an initial direction  
- communicating your thinking  

Milestones:
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Project Storyboard (Draft 1)  
- Intro / Project Overview Video  

Note:

You do not need to have the right answer yet.

You should think the problem through, explore the data, and choose a reasonable direction.  
Be aware of alternative approaches and be prepared to adjust as you learn more.

---

### Phase 3: Exploration and Alignment (Weeks 4–6)

Focus:
- refining your direction  
- working with stakeholders  
- improving technical setup  

Milestones:
- Outreach  
- Install Instructions  

---

### Phase 4: Build and Explain (Weeks 6–9)

Focus:
- developing your solution  
- testing ideas  
- explaining your approach  

Milestones:
- Project Report (Draft 1)  
- MVP Video (Demo Walkthrough)  

---

### Phase 5: Refine and Make Reproducible (Weeks 9–11)

Focus:
- improving your project  
- organizing and reproducing your work  
- refining communication  

Milestones:
- Reproducibility  
- Project Report (Draft 2 – Peer Review)  

---

### Phase 6: Final Integration (Weeks 11–End)

Focus:
- producing a clear, complete project  
- combining technical work and communication  

Milestones:
- Final Project (Report + Final Video)  

---

## How the Pieces Fit Together

You are building one project, not completing separate assignments.

Over time:

- **EDA informs your understanding of the problem**  
- **Your storyboard evolves into your final video**  
- **Your report drafts evolve into your final paper**  
- **Your code evolves into a reproducible system**  

---

## Weekly Structure

Each week supports steady progress:

- **Weekly milestones** → build and communicate progress  
- **3×3 reflections** → surface perspectives and questions  
- **Check-ins** → maintain accountability  
- **Team charter** → guide how your team responds to challenges  

These are not separate tasks. They are a system.

---

## Working Ahead and Iterating

The roadmap provides structure, but it is not a limit.

- If something is working, continue building on it  
- If something is not working, spend time iterating and improving  

Weekly milestones define a **minimum level of progress**, not the maximum.

You are encouraged to:
- think ahead  
- start work early  
- revisit and improve earlier work  

Progress is not linear. Adjusting your direction is part of the process.

---

## Bottom Line

Projects do not start with answers.

They start with exploration, communication, and thoughtful decisions about where to go next.

Successful teams:
- explore early  
- communicate clearly  
- choose a direction  
- and improve through iteration
