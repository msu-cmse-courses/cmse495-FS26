# Final Reflection

This reflection is your opportunity to describe what you learned, how you contributed, and what advice you would give to future students.

## Prompt

Write a concise reflection (about 1-2 pages) that addresses the following:

1. **Contributions**
- What were your main contributions (technical, organizational, communication)?
- How did your contributions affect project outcomes?

2. **Learning and Growth**
- What did you learn about data science project work in real contexts?
- How did your approach change over the semester?
- What professional habits did you develop?

3. **Challenges and Successes**
- What were your major challenges?
- How did you and your team respond?
- What are you most proud of?

4. **Advice to Future Students**
- What do you wish you had known in Week 1?
- What two pieces of practical advice would you share?

## Expectations

- Use your own words
- Be specific and reflective, not generic
- Connect your reflection to concrete project/team experiences

## Submission Details

- **Format:** Typed, professional tone
- **Length:** Approximately 1-2 pages
- **Due Date / Platform:** See course schedule and instructor directions
