# CMSE 495 Project Showcase
### Part of Michigan State University (MSU) Engineering Design Day

**Date:** Friday, December 11, 2026  
**Time:** 10:20 AM - 12:10 PM  
**Location:** Engineering Building, Room 1145

For more information about MSU Engineering Design Day, visit:

<https://designday.msu.edu/>

## Finding the Engineering Building

The Engineering Building is located at the southeast corner of Red Cedar Road and South Shaw Lane.

**Room 1145** is located at the end of the shorter north-south hallway within the Engineering Building.

## Parking

Two visitor pay-by-plate parking lots are within walking distance of the Engineering Building:

- **Lot 39**: Located directly across from the Engineering Building on South Shaw Lane.
- **Lot 79**: Located on the south side of Spartan Stadium on North Shaw Lane.

Additional information on visitor parking and rates can be found at:

* <https://parking.msu.edu/visitor-parking>

## Campus Maps

- [Design Day Directions page](https://designday.msu.edu/maps-directions) 


# Agenda

The **CMSE Project Showcase** will be held in the Engineering building room 1145 and is open to the public.

### Round A 10:30-11:10am
1. MDCR - Michigan Department of Civil Rights: Contractor EEO Compliance Analytics
2. MSU-Qualtrics - Qualtrics Data Collection Automation and Analysis
3. NC3 - Lansing Economic Ecosystem and Community Wealth Analysis
4. NIST-CR-NLP - NIST Community Resilience NLP
   
### Round B 11:20-12:00pm
1. MSU-Fruit - Scalable Fruit Yield Estimation Using Video-Based Computer Vision
2. Niles-SA - Niles Scaffolding and Coatings Service Analytics
3. MxV-Wheel_Profile - Identifying Wheel Profile Characteristics Causing Hunting


### Timeline (110 Minutes)
1. **Welcome & Setup** – 10 minutes  
2. **Lightning Talks (Round A)** – 20 minutes
3. **Discussion and Questions** – 20 minutes
4. **Team Switch** - 10 minutes
5. **Lightning Talks (Round B)** – 20 minutes  
6. **Discussion and Questions** – 20 minutes
7. **Wrap-Up & Closing Remarks** – 10 minutes
