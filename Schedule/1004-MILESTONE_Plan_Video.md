# Project Proposal Video

For this assignment you and your team will create and submit a 3-5 minute project Introduction video. Tap into your own creativity. This video will be given to your community partners.  Make something you are proud of and would want to post on-line or include in a presentation. 

For more information please see the [Project Videos](../Guide/Project_Videos) page in the Course Guide.

We will use time in class on Monday to watch the videos, answer questions and provide feedback.

**_NOTE_**:  Your instructor assumes that you are well past the planning stage and are actively working on closing your open loops. 


## Submission

Put two copies of your video in the class team drive; one inside your private team folder and one in your public team folder in the general channel. 
 

## Evaluation

Videos will be graded for professionalism and completeness.  Instructors will count off points for poor quality sound or visualizations. This video will specifically want to see a introduction to the project and a clear plan for success.  

 - 40 Video Content and Completeness
 - 30 Professionalism and Presentation
 - 20 Audio / Visual Quality
 - 10 Proper Submission and Sharing
