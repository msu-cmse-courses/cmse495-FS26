# Weekly Routine

Use this page as the weekly map. Each day links to the detailed assignment guides.

## Weekly Flow

- **Monday:** [Peer Review and Team Planning](Mondays_Peer_Review_and_Planning)
- **Wednesday:** [CyberAmbassadors](Wednesday_CyberAmbassadors)
- **Friday:** [SCHOLAR Technical Training](Fridays_SCHOLARS)

## Milestone Deadline Policy

- Milestones for the full semester are shared during Week 1
- One milestone is due each week on **Sunday**
- Milestones are due on time with **no extensions**

For milestone details and sequence, use [Milestone Roadmap](Milestone-Roadmap).

## Monday Checklist (Quick View)

- Complete required peer review tasks
- Submit and discuss your [Weekly 3x3](Weekly-3x3)
- Update your [Meeting Agenda and Notes](Meeting-Agenda_and_Notes)
- Confirm team goals and owners for the week

## Why This Structure

Weekly Routine gives you the cadence; linked pages provide the detailed expectations.
