# Project Preferences

During the first week of class you are expected to review project proposals and provide information about yourself and your preferences so that the instructors can make team assignments.  Note that not everyone will get their first or even their second project choices.  However, your instructors will do their best to build teams that match your interests and have the skills necessary to be successful.


1. **_Review Project Descriptions_** 
    - [Link to Microsoft Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/team/19%3AxMKs9Zrf34yn3XEn5AWyStX2erP0l0HXoNy1pQ322Rc1%40thread.tacv2/conversations?groupId=45421c5f-71aa-4ae9-80aa-2cb3c3d3a140&tenantId=22177130-642f-41d9-9211-74237ad5687d) - Project Descriptions will be in the "General" channel under files.
    - **_NOTE_** Not all files may be available until the first day of class.
3. **_Specify your project preferences_** Fill out the following survey document to specify your preferences. 
    - [Project Survey](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/yjb0Axfbb3)

You must complete all steps by the due date (Sunday August 6, 2026) in order to get credit for this assignment.  Your instructor will try to arrange the teams by class on Wednesday.  (Reminder that there is no class on Monday due to a University Holiday).
