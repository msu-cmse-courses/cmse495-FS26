# Daily Class Check-In

## Overview (TLDR)

The Daily Check-In is a quick reflection on your participation and professionalism in class.

- Submit once each class day  
- Reflect honestly on your engagement  
- Communicate with instructors when things do not go as planned  

👉 Complete the check-in here: https://msu.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3yNg09psIE2ObAO

## Why We Do This

The Daily Check-In is part of how we build professional habits in this course.

Rather than taking attendance, this system is based on trust and self-reporting.

It gives you a structured way to:
- reflect on how you showed up to class  
- communicate directly with the instructors  
- take ownership of your engagement and progress  

## What This Is (and Is Not)

This check-in is not about monitoring or policing behavior.

It is used to:
- encourage reflection on your level of engagement  
- provide visibility to instructors  
- build a culture of trust and accountability  

## Expectations

You are expected to:

- submit a check-in each class day  
- report your participation honestly  
- communicate when you are late or less engaged  
- take responsibility for staying on track  

If something does not go as planned, the goal is not perfection—it is awareness and follow-through.

## Important Note

Planned absences or schedule changes should be reported separately using the Planned Absence form.

The Daily Check-In reflects what actually happened during class.
